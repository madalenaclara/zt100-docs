Once the foundations are in place, the platform builds agents, tools and automations that remove manual work, designed around a client's real bottlenecks.

![Three kinds of build: internal AI tools, autonomous agents, and document handling with on-device AI (demo data).](/images/agents.svg)
_Internal tools, always-on agents, and document and on-device AI for sensitive work (demo data)._

## What it builds
- **Internal AI tools**: custom interfaces, dashboards and assistants for a specific job.
- **Autonomous agents**: always-on agents that prepare work before it is asked for, such as research, monitoring and reporting.
- **Document handling & on-device AI**: document scraping, RPA, private transcription and offline AI for sensitive work.

## How it fits with Private AI
Private AI and Agents work together but are not the same part:
- **Private AI** is the secure environment, the place where the organisation's data lives and AI runs inside its own perimeter.
- **Agents & automation** is the build layer, the agents, tools and automations that do the work. They can run inside Private AI for sensitive data, or in the tools the organisation already uses.

## Good to know
This is where AI stops helping with the work and starts doing it: implementation projects plus recurring, managed-service revenue the partner can own.
