The platform aggregates the mapped workflows into an organisation-wide view: which business processes are most automatable, how many people they affect, and how many weekly hours could be recovered.

![AI Use Cases: weekly hours recoverable and the most automatable processes, ranked with people impacted and time lost (demo data).](/images/usecases.jpg)
_Headline numbers and a ranked table of business processes by how automatable they are, with people impacted, time lost per week and a governance flag (demo data)._

## What you can see
- **Headline numbers**: workflows analysed, average automatable work, total weekly hours, and weekly hours recoverable.
- A ranked list of processes by how automatable they are, with people impacted and time lost per week.
- A step-by-step breakdown and suggested tools for each process, with governance flags such as "consult IT governance".

## Good to know
The hours-recoverable figure sizes the opportunity, and the ranked list tells you which processes to tackle first. It turns scattered workflow maps into one prioritised plan a leadership team can act on.
