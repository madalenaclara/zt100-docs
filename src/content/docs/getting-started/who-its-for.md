Zero to 100 is for leaders who want a clear, measurable path to AI across the organisation, not a pile of disconnected tools and one-off training.

It is used with companies of any size and delivered by partners who own the client relationship. The partner runs the programme; Zero to 100 provides the platform, the learning content, the deployments and the expert backing.

## Good fits
- Organisations where AI use is growing but uneven, with no visibility and no standards.
- Regulated or security-conscious teams that need private AI and governance.
- Leaders who want to turn AI from scattered experiments into measurable productivity.

## Less of a fit
- Teams looking for a single chatbot licence rather than an adoption programme.
- Buyers who want a tool to self-serve with no partner and no rollout.

## How it is delivered
A partner sets up the platform, reads the diagnostics with the client's leadership, assigns the learning, scopes the build and automation work, and re-assesses to show progress. Escalation items such as pricing, contracts, data residency, security and custom integrations are handled directly with Zero to 100.
