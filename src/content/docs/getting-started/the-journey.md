---
title: The journey
description: Assess, learn, build, deploy, then again.
---

Each part of the platform does one job. Used together they form a loop.

1. **Assess** — measure where people stand.
2. **Learn** — close the gaps.
3. **Build** — turn real tasks into working assistants.
4. **Deploy** — put AI to work where it matters.

Re-assess over time to track progress. Each person sees how they have changed since the last result.
