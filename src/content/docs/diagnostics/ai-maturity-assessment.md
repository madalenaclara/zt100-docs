---
title: AI Maturity Assessment
description: A short assessment that measures AI capability.
---

A 10 to 15 minute assessment that every employee takes. It measures five practical skills (prompting, tool use, responsible use, co-intelligence and AI mastery) and rolls them up into a maturity picture across four dimensions: skills, organisational change, strategy and governance, each on a five-stage scale from planning to leading.

## What you can see
- A score for each person, each department and the whole organisation.
- The five skills on a radar, current performance against a target.
- A strategy-versus-capability map of leaders, builders, planners and beginners.
