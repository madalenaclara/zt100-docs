An optional, light-touch browser extension that records which AI tools are used across the organisation, in real time, without getting in anyone's way.

![The extension popup showing AI tools in use this week, each flagged approved or unauthorised, syncing to Risk & Compliance (demo data).](/images/browser-extension.svg)
_The extension detects AI tools in the background and flags each as approved or unauthorised, feeding the Risk & Compliance view (demo data)._

## What it does
- Shows which AI tools each team uses, and how much.
- Gives a real picture of adoption: what people actually do, beyond what the assessment reports.
- Flags use of tools that are not approved, feeding the Risk & Compliance view.

## Assessment vs extension
- The **assessment** shows what people *can* do, by skill and dimension.
- The **extension** shows what people *actually* do, tool by tool, week by week.
- Together they give a complete, honest picture: capability and real behaviour side by side.

## Good to know
It is light-touch by design: detection runs in the background and never interrupts the person's work. Rollout is optional, so a client can lead with the assessment and add the extension when they want the live adoption picture.
