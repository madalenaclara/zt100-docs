The same assessment data powers a governance view. The platform scores the organisation's AI risk, breaks it down by department, and detects which AI tools people are actually using, flagging each as approved or not against a list you control.

![Risk & Compliance: an AI risk score, risk by department, and detected AI tools flagged approved or unauthorised (demo data).](/images/risk.jpg)
_AI risk exposure, high-risk departments, approved tools and shadow-AI users, with a per-department risk heatmap and the detected tools (demo data)._

## What you can see
- An overall **AI risk score**, based on the assessments.
- A **risk heatmap by department**, ranked from lowest to highest risk.
- A **Shadow AI Tools overview**: which tools are in use (ChatGPT, Copilot, Claude, Gemini, NotebookLM, Perplexity and others), their usage, risk level and approval status.
- A count of shadow-AI users: people using tools that are not on the approved list.

## Good to know
This is the natural way in at security-conscious and regulated clients: lead with governance and the shadow-AI picture, then expand into enablement. The approved-tools list is yours to control, so the same view also doubles as a policy record.
