A hands-on space where people practise and build, separate from the courses. It is where a skill becomes real work before anything is rolled out.

![The AI Lab: a Prompt Library, a Prompt Sandbox and the Prompt Challenge Arena (demo data).](/images/ai-lab.svg)
_Hands-on tools that turn training into real, tested work (demo data)._

## What's inside
- **Prompt Library**: 50+ ready-to-use prompts across business functions, with personalised recommendations.
- **Prompt Sandbox**: practise and test prompts against different AI models.
- **Prompt Challenge Arena**: practise prompt engineering on realistic business scenarios.

## Good to know
The AI Lab is what turns training into adoption, and it surfaces the concrete use cases that become build and automation projects.
