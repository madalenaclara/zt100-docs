Two kinds of course, so a person can follow a full path or pick up a single skill.

![The catalogue: structured learning journeys by level, and a set of short micro-skills (demo data).](/images/courses.svg)
_Learning journeys by maturity level with progress and certification, plus a catalogue of short micro-skills (demo data)._

## Learning journeys
Structured, self-paced paths matched to someone's maturity level, for example AI Fluency, Building Your AI Assistants, Automating Your Work with AI Workflows, and Building Prototypes with AI. They use real workplace scenarios and end in certification.

## Micro-skills
Short, specific lessons on one task, kept up to date as the tools change, for example working with Claude, building a Custom GPT, setting up a Copilot agent, or creating a podcast. Ideal when someone needs one thing quickly, in their own role and language.

## What you can see
- Learning journeys by maturity level, assigned from the assessment results.
- A catalogue of micro-skills people can complete in minutes.
- Completion, certification and progress tracking, in several languages.

## Good to know
Because the assessment assigns the right level automatically, people start where they actually are, and the catalogue stays current as the tools change.
