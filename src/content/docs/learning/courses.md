---
title: "Courses: learning journeys & micro-skills"
description: "Two kinds of course."
---

Two kinds of course, so a person can follow a path or pick up a single skill.

**Learning journeys** are structured, self-paced paths matched to someone's maturity level.

**Micro-skills** are short, specific lessons on one task, kept up to date as the tools change, for example building a Custom GPT or setting up a Copilot agent.
