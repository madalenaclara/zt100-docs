Answers to the questions we hear most often.

## What is Zero to 100?
One platform that takes an organisation from where it is today to using AI across everyday work. It measures readiness, teaches the missing skills, helps people turn real tasks into assistants, and deploys private AI where it is needed. See [What is Zero to 100](/).

## Who delivers it?
Partners. A partner owns the client relationship and runs the programme; Zero to 100 provides the platform, the learning content, the deployments and the expert backing. See [Who it's for](/getting-started/who-its-for/).

## How long does the assessment take?
10 to 15 minutes per employee. It measures five practical skills and rolls them up into a maturity picture. It is built to be repeated so progress can be tracked. See [AI Maturity Assessment](/diagnostics/ai-maturity-assessment/).

## Is our data private?
The Private AI deployment runs inside the client's own private cloud or on-premise, with audit logs, role-based access and policy controls, and data is never shared with an external provider. See [Private deployment & controls](/private-ai/private-deployment/).

## What about tools people already use, like ChatGPT or Copilot?
The platform detects which AI tools are in use and flags each as approved or not against a list you control, so shadow AI is visible rather than hidden. See [Risk & Compliance](/diagnostics/risk-compliance/).

## What languages are supported?
The learning content and assessment are available in several languages, and micro-skills are delivered in the person's own role and language.

## How is pricing structured?
Commercials, contracts, data residency, security and custom integrations are handled directly with your Zero to 100 contact. Reach out to your partner for a quote scoped to your organisation.
