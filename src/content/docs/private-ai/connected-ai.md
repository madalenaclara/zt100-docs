title: Connected AI across your work
description: One private AI layer across email, calendar, meetings, documents and workflows.

Most AI tools start from zero every session. They do not know the person's meetings, inbox or work, so the output is generic and someone has to re-explain the context each time. Private AI takes the opposite approach: one AI layer connected across email, calendar, meetings, documents, knowledge bases and internal systems, sharing a single context so it already knows the person's work. Drafts, summaries and answers are about them and their organisation, and nothing leaves the environment.

## What it connects
- **Inbox**: a smart inbox that sorts what needs a reply and drafts responses in the person's voice.
- **Calendar and meetings**: upcoming meetings, briefings prepared in advance, and notes taken automatically.
- **Drive**: the organisation's documents, transcripts and workflow outputs, used to ground every answer.
- **Chat**: one place to ask across all of it, with the option to pull in a specific email, meeting or file, search the web, or run deeper research.
- **Workflows**: recurring work described once and then run on a schedule.

## What it removes
- Re-explaining context to AI every session.
- Switching between several tools to get one thing done.
- Taking meeting notes by hand instead of listening.

## Good to know
This is the compliant alternative to public Copilot or ChatGPT: the same everyday help, grounded in the organisation's own work, running inside its own environment. The more it is used, the more context it has, so the output keeps getting more specific over time.
